## Overview

GreenGrid Analytics is a comprehensive renewable energy intelligence dashboard built using **Python**, **Streamlit**, and **Plotly**. It enables users to explore renewable energy adoption, carbon emissions, electricity demand, clean energy investments, and sustainability indicators through interactive visualizations and insightful analytics.

---

## Features

* 📊 Interactive KPI Dashboard
* 🌍 Global Renewable Energy Analysis
* 🗺️ World Choropleth Map
* 📈 Renewable Energy Trend Analysis
* 💰 Investment Analytics
* 🌞 Renewable Energy Mix Visualization
* 🌳 Treemap & Sunburst Charts
* 🫧 Interactive Scatter & Bubble Charts
* 📉 Carbon Intensity Analysis
* 🏆 Executive Insights
* 📄 Country Performance Summary
* 📥 Download Filtered Dataset
* 📋 Interactive Dataset Preview
* 🌙 Professional Dark Theme
* 🎛️ Dynamic Filters for Year, Continent, and Country

---

## Dataset Information

* **Records:** 3,000
* **Features:** 25
* **Countries:** 30
* **Continents:** 6
* **Years Covered:** 2005–2025

The dataset includes renewable energy generation, electricity demand, GDP, renewable investments, carbon emissions, energy storage capacity, electricity prices, EV adoption, clean energy scores, and sustainability indicators.
